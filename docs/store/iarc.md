# IARC age-rating questionnaire — answers

Product: MeetBurner (Windows desktop app, EXE/MSI).

Use this document to fill the IARC questionnaire in Partner Center. The exact wording of the
questions changes, but the answers below cover every content category the questionnaire asks about.

## Short description for the questionnaire

A productivity utility that shows the running cost of a meeting and can publish the counter as a
virtual camera. It has no account, no user-to-user communication, no purchases, no ads and no
personal data collection.

| Age rating | Everyone / 3+ |

## Why

- The user enters participant names, roles or salaries on their own device. Nothing is uploaded
  and there is no service, profile or account.
- A share link is created by the user on demand and carries the meeting summary in the URL
  fragment. It is not posted anywhere by MeetBurner; the user decides whether to send it.
- There is no chat, no comments, no purchases, no ads and no personal data collection.
- The desktop app makes no network calls and has no external links.
- The Store screenshots include a clearly labelled schematic that illustrates the camera overlay.
  It is an illustration, not gameplay or user-generated content.

The resulting rating should be Everyone (3+). If the questionnaire shows a different minimum for
utility apps in a region, keep the content answers above and choose the lowest age.
