## Product name

MeetBurner

## Short description

See what every meeting costs, live — and put the counter on your camera.

## Description

MeetBurner shows what a meeting costs while it happens.

Add the people in the room, pick their salaries (or type your own) and press Start. The counter
shows the total cost, the elapsed time, the combined person-time and the cost per minute, updating
in real time. When the meeting ends, share a link with the summary.

The Windows app can publish the counter as a virtual camera. Choose MeetBurner Camera in Zoom,
Teams, Google Meet in Chrome or Edge, OBS or any other app that reads a camera, and the meeting
sees the cost as your video. On Windows 11, MeetBurner also registers MeetBurner Camera (Windows
11), a Media Foundation camera for the new Teams and the Windows Camera app.

If you want your face and the counter together, add a video background: MeetBurner composites the
counter card over your webcam. Drag the card to move it, use the corner handle to resize it and
the round handle to turn it. The camera image and the card can be mirrored independently.

Make it yours:

- Seven counter styles, from Classic and Formal to Halloween, Christmas, 4 seasons and
  Minimalist, plus Split-flap, the airport board look where every character sits on a plate and
  flips when it changes.
- Give each text element its own font, size and colour, or hide it.
- Solid or gradient background, with opacity and corner radius.
- Large amounts abbreviate (K, M, B) or shrink the font to fit.

No account, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reporting. The webcam is used only on your
device; the desktop app talks to no network service. Settings stay on your device. Share links
carry the summary in the URL fragment, which browsers do not send to a server.

English and Spanish, in the app and in the counter.

Requirements: Windows 10 (x64) or later; the Store installer includes the WebView2 runtime; a
webcam for the video background; Windows 11 for MeetBurner Camera (Windows 11). The salaries are
estimates you can edit; MeetBurner is not financial advice.
